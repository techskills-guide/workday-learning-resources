Workday Learning LMS: Courses, Programs and Reporting

Understand Workday Learning LMS, course catalogs, learning programs, compliance tracking, integrations and useful concepts for beginners.

Workday Learning is used by organizations to organize learning content and support employee development. Its setup and available functions depend on the products and configuration used by an organization. For beginners, the best starting point is to understand how learning content, assignments, audiences and completion records fit together.

Core Workday Learning concepts

Courses and content

A course organizes learning content and activities around a topic or objective. Content may include documents, videos or supported e-learning packages. Understand the learner's experience, prerequisites and how completion is recorded.

Programs and learning paths

Programs or structured learning paths can group learning activities into a sequence. They may be used for onboarding, role-based development or required training. The exact terms and capabilities depend on configuration and current product functionality.

Campaigns and assignments

Learning teams may assign or promote learning to particular audiences. When studying this area, consider audience criteria, due dates, notifications, exemptions and how administrators review progress.

Reporting and compliance

Reports can help learning administrators review enrollment, progress, completion and compliance-related activity. Good reporting starts with a clear question, consistent definitions and appropriate data access.

Security and integrations

Security determines who can manage learning content and view learner information. Integrations may connect learning workflows with HCM data, content providers or other systems. Always confirm supported interfaces and security requirements for the specific environment.

Example: planning an onboarding learning program

  1. Define the audience, such as new employees in a particular role.
  2. Identify required and optional learning content.
  3. Arrange the learning activities into a sensible sequence.
  4. Define completion criteria and due dates.
  5. Decide which reports the learning team needs and who is authorized to see them.
  6. Test the learner and administrator experience using an approved environment before rollout.
Avoid putting real employee data into unapproved test files or sharing learner records outside authorized channels.

Skills worth practising

  • Learning catalog and content organization.
  • Course and program design principles.
  • Learning assignments and completion tracking.
  • Reports, filters and learning analytics.
  • Security roles, troubleshooting and release awareness.

How to choose a learning resource

Look for a syllabus that includes configuration concepts, realistic exercises, reporting and troubleshooting rather than only feature descriptions. You can review the Workday LMS Training Program page as one course option, and verify its current curriculum, schedule and terms directly.
